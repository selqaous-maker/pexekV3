# PEXEK Global Homepage V3.1 — Design Direction

## Three visual approaches considered

### Approach 1
**Theme Name:** Signal Atelier

**Very Brief Intro:** A calm editorial operations studio: warm white space, restrained teal signals and carefully structured workflow diagrams. It makes AI feel accountable, considered and useful rather than loud or futuristic.

**Probability:** 0.07

### Approach 2
**Theme Name:** Night Control Room

**Very Brief Intro:** A dark technical dashboard aesthetic with luminous signal paths, data-grid texture and high-contrast controls. It feels powerful but risks looking like a generic AI/cyberpunk startup.

**Probability:** 0.04

### Approach 3
**Theme Name:** Business Field Notes

**Very Brief Intro:** A documentary-inspired direction combining real industry imagery with annotated notes and tactile paper cues. It feels human and grounded but can become too informal for a global B2B solution.

**Probability:** 0.09

## Chosen direction: Signal Atelier Midnight

### Design Movement

Contemporary Swiss editorial design combined with Japanese information design. The page should feel like a premium operating manual for customer conversations: clear, calm, deliberate and evidence-minded.

### Core Principles

1. **Clarity before spectacle:** Every visual element should explain a business handoff, channel or decision.
2. **Human control is visible:** The design should make the human handoff feel central rather than hidden behind automation.
3. **Calm confidence:** Use space, editorial hierarchy and subtle craftsmanship instead of loud AI effects.
4. **One system, many contexts:** Global positioning is expressed through a shared visual workflow, while industries remain a secondary route.

### Color Philosophy

The PEXEK original logo establishes the core palette: deep midnight navy, electric cyan and luminous blue. The page uses navy for authority and contrast, electric cyan for active signal routes and CTAs, and a restrained apricot accent only for human handoff. Pale ice-blue sections preserve readability and keep the result premium rather than generic cyberpunk.

### Layout Paradigm

Use an asymmetric editorial column rather than a centered SaaS landing-page stack. The hero uses a left narrative column and a right “signal route” visual. Later sections alternate between wide statements, side annotations and connected workflow panels.

### Signature Elements

1. **Signal route:** A thin teal path moves through channel, qualification, next-action and human nodes.
2. **Margin notes:** Small numbered labels and short operational annotations sit at the edge of major sections.
3. **Human handoff marker:** A warm apricot node indicates when responsibility moves to the client team.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should explain, not entertain. Buttons have a crisp active press. Workflow nodes reveal one practical sentence on hover or focus. The assessment CTA scrolls to the form instead of opening a distracting modal.

### Animation

Use only transform and opacity animations. On load, the editorial copy appears first, then the signal route draws in with a short stagger. Sections enter with 20–40px vertical movement and 180–260ms custom easing. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System

Use **Space Grotesk** for headings, labels and numbers; use **DM Sans** for body copy and form labels. Headings are compact and deliberate; body copy is wide enough to read without looking like a dense product spec. Do not use Inter.

### Brand Essence

**Positioning:** PEXEK turns inbound customer conversations into clear, human-owned next actions for businesses that cannot afford to lose context.

**Personality:** Precise, composed, accountable.

### Brand Voice

Headlines should sound operational and confident, never generic or hype-driven. CTAs should explain the next step rather than pressure the visitor.

Examples:

> Turn customer conversations into clear next actions.

> See how your first response becomes a better handoff.

### Wordmark & Logo

Use the approved original PEXEK geometric emblem as the primary mark in header, footer and favicon. The wordmark sits beside it in Space Grotesk; no descriptive tagline is embedded in the logo.

### Signature Brand Color

**Electric PEXEK Cyan — #18C9FF**

## Implementation reminder

Every page, component and stylesheet edited for this project must carry a concise comment referencing **Signal Atelier Midnight: editorial clarity, PEXEK navy/cyan signal route, human handoff, calm premium B2B**. When in doubt, ask: “Does this choice reinforce or dilute the Signal Atelier Midnight philosophy?”

## Style Decisions

The signal route is a system-wide motif on every major section. It connects demand, context, next action and the human handoff, and must always clarify the operating model rather than decorate it.

Apricot is reserved exclusively for human ownership: team confirmation, commercial judgement, feasibility, price, stock, negotiation and final decision.

The PEXEK lockup is the approved geometric emblem plus the PEXEK wordmark in Space Grotesk. Product descriptors belong in navigation or page copy, never inside the logo lockup.
